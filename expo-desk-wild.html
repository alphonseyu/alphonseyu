FromLecturerToEvents Module EXCODate29 July 2010SubjectMascot responsibilities

Hi everyone

I'd like Alphonse to be free of Mascot responsibilities.

You may not have noticed, but at all the events (CCN day, Suntec etc), Alphonse's the guy who works in the background to ensure things run smoothly.

At Suntec, while everyone was busy getting pebble pledges, Alphonse was working behind the scenes to ensure pebbles and numbers were in order. He watched out for little details like children approaching the display and assisted / carried them to a "safe" part where they could take photos. He was the "security" and was selective about children who wanted to see the pebbles in the water. An autistic boy was fretting and Alphonse guided him into the area and patiently explained the project, at the same time making sure the boy stood back and watched his safety.

Alphonse's sharp and has a knack of picking up details and his observation is crucial on the day because everyone will be running around and pre-occupied with their own duties. You'll need someone who's calm to take a step back and view things objectively.

If anyone has an issue, ask them to speak to me.

The Mascot will not be on duty the whole session. I expect them to take breaks in between to rest. See how you can rework the duties but I want Alphonse to concentrate on the notebook/slide show/online updates on FB and twitter etc.
